# AJJI RUCHI MASALA POWDERS

AJJI RUCHI MASALA POWDERS is a Flutter-based e-commerce application designed to bring authentic Indian masala powders to customers through a simple and user-friendly online shopping experience.

### Features
- Browse products by category and view product details.
- Add products to the cart and proceed to checkout.
- Support for UPI and Cash on Delivery (COD) payment options.
- Firebase Firestore integration for product and order data.
- Admin dashboard for managing products and tracking customer orders.

### Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** Flutter, Dart
- **Backend & Database:** Firebase Firestore
- **State Management:** Provider
- **Navigation:** GoRouter

### Goal
To provide a convenient online shopping platform that combines traditional flavours with a modern digital experience.
